# Predictions

[Português](predictions.pt-BR.md) · [Notebook](README.md)

What we think AI will do, written before it happens. Each prediction has who made it, when, one sentence that can be checked without argument, and the date to check it. On that date we reread it and grade it: **right**, **wrong** or **too early**, with the reason. A prediction is never deleted or rewritten afterwards: that is what gives the grade its value.

The goal is not to be right. It is to learn to tell what AI actually does from what is promised, our own promises included.
