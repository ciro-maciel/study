# Social networks (1997 to 2006)

[Português](social-networks.pt-BR.md) · [Brain](../README.md) · Previous: [web](web.md) · Next: [artificial intelligence](ai.md)

**In one sentence:** social networks made it almost free to reach people and be found by them, and swapped the page we visit for the flow (the feed) that comes to us.

## What existed before

The web linked documents. To hear from a person you had to go to their page, if they had one. Reaching many people still required being a media outlet.

## The leap

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| 1997 | SixDegrees.com, the first service with profiles and friend lists; it closes in 2000 |
| 4 Feb 2004 | Mark Zuckerberg launches "TheFacebook" at Harvard |
| 2006 | Facebook creates News Feed: instead of visiting profiles, everyone's news arrives in a single column |

## The analysis

- **What became cheap:** reaching people. An ordinary person could now speak to thousands without a newspaper, radio or TV.
- **The open layer:** here is the difference. Social networks run on open layers (the internet, the web), but **they are closed themselves**: each person's profile, contacts and reach belong to the company. It is the first leap on this list where the middle layer is not common.
- **The interface for people:** the feed. Nobody has to look for anything; content arrives, chosen by an algorithm.
- **What it connected:** **people**, not documents. And each new person makes the network more valuable for everyone else (the network effect).
- **The bad side:** attention became the product, and the algorithm is optimized to hold, not to inform. Privacy was dealt with afterwards (the Cambridge Analytica case, in 2018). The effect on mental health and polarization is still being studied and debated.
