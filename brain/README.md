# The brain

[Português](README.pt-BR.md) · [Repository](../README.md)

A shared memory between **Ciro Cesar Maciel** and **Claude** for three questions: **how humanity took its great leaps**, **what those leaps have in common** and **what the next one is**, along with what the two of us can build to help take it.

Ciro brings what he finds: a video, a paper, a conversation, an idea in the shower. Claude reads it, links it to what is already here and keeps everything current. Together, we process and extrapolate.

## Where the idea comes from

In July 1945, Vannevar Bush described the [Memex](leaps/memex.md): a desk where a person would keep everything they read, linked by trails of association. The web linked the world's documents, but the part nobody solved was **who maintains the links** as knowledge grows. People abandon that work; a language model does not tire of it. This brain is an attempt to close the loop Bush opened.

## How it is organized

| Folder | What it holds | Who writes it |

## How we use it

- **Bring.** Ciro sends something with one line on why. Claude reads it, summarizes what matters, updates the leap pages it touches, revisits the patterns and says whether any frontier hypothesis got stronger or weaker.
- **Ask.** Any question to the brain is answered from the pages, with sources. A good answer becomes a page instead of getting lost in the chat.
- **Extrapolate.** Every so often, a frontier-only session: which pattern points where, what changed, what is worth building now.
- **Audit.** Claude looks for what went stale, what contradicts something else and what is mentioned without a page of its own.

## Rules

- Every statement of fact has a source. Opinion and hypothesis are marked as such.
- The bad side of each leap goes in with the good.
- Nothing here promises the future: the frontier is hypotheses, with the signals that would refute them.
