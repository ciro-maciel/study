# Frontier

[Português](README.pt-BR.md) · [Brain](../README.md)

Hypotheses for the next leap. Each one is a bet, not a prediction: it says which [patterns](../patterns/README.md) point to it, what can be seen today, **what would refute it** and what the two of us can build. A hypothesis is never deleted; when the signals refute it, it gets the date and the reason, and stays here as a lesson. First reading: 31 October 2026.

## 1. Collective memory kept by AI

**The hypothesis.** The [Memex](../leaps/memex.md) of 1945 becomes real: knowledge curated by people and kept by models, which read everything that comes in, link it to what exists, flag contradictions and keep it current. Not for one person: for groups, companies, cities, humanity.

**The patterns.** The idea is 80 years old and was waiting for a cost to fall ([pattern 2](../patterns/README.md#2-the-idea-comes-decades-early)); the cost is that of maintaining the links, which a model does almost for free ([pattern 1](../patterns/README.md#1-a-cost-collapses)). The open layer already exists: plain text, links and version control ([pattern 3](../patterns/README.md#3-an-open-common-layer)).

**What can be seen.** The "llm-wiki" pattern described by Andrej Karpathy; Wikipedia as proof that the encyclopedia with trails works when someone maintains it; this brain itself.

**What would refute it.** If AI-kept memories pile up errors faster than people can correct them, trust disappears and the idea goes back in the drawer.

**What we can build.** This brain, as open proof that it works. Then the same format offered to those who need it: a memory for agents, a company memory, the memory of a field of knowledge.

## 2. Agents that work for people

**The hypothesis.** [AI](../leaps/ai.md) leaves the chat and goes to work: agents that carry out whole tasks with tools, under written rules about what they may do. A person's work becomes saying what they want, deciding and checking.

**The patterns.** The cost that falls is that of doing ([pattern 1](../patterns/README.md#1-a-cost-collapses)); the open layer is the protocols connecting models to tools, such as the Model Context Protocol ([pattern 3](../patterns/README.md#3-an-open-common-layer)); and each agent enlarges the market for those who coordinate agents ([pattern 7](../patterns/README.md#7-the-market-grows-and-work-divides-further)).

**What would refute it.** If agents' mistakes in the real world (money, health, data) become too costly to make up for the time saved.

**What we can build.** Agents with a constitution: a short list of principles, written by the business owner, that the program checks before each action. Join the notebook's [agents](../../notebook/concepts/agents/README.md) and [constitution](../../notebook/concepts/constitution/README.md) pages.

## 3. Knowledge in everyone's language

**The hypothesis.** The world's knowledge becomes equally accessible in any language: asking, reading and learning in Portuguese costs the same and finds as much as in English.

**The patterns.** Today the market for knowledge in Portuguese is too small to sustain those who would specialize in it ([pattern 7](../patterns/README.md#7-the-market-grows-and-work-divides-further)). And the cost is still unequal: on Claude, the same text costs 16% more tokens in Portuguese ([notebook, token page](../../notebook/concepts/token/README.md)).

**What would refute it.** If English becomes the single language of knowledge for good and machine translation is enough for everything else.

**What we can build.** First-rate knowledge written in Portuguese, from the inside: this brain, the notebook, the books of the *O Novo Básico* series. It is the advantage of thinking in Portuguese, not a translation.

## 4. General-purpose robots

**The hypothesis.** The robot stops doing one task in a fixed place and starts understanding what needs doing in places it has never seen, directed by words, like AI.

**The patterns.** The idea is a hundred years old ([pattern 2](../patterns/README.md#2-the-idea-comes-decades-early)); what is missing is the interface ([pattern 4](../patterns/README.md#4-the-leap-happens-at-the-interface)) and a common layer ([pattern 3](../patterns/README.md#3-an-open-common-layer)), which does not exist yet.

**What would refute it.** If the physical world proves too varied for models trained on text and video, and a robot's cost stays above a person's for many years.

**What we can build.** For now, watch: it is the leap furthest from what we do today. It stays here so the signals are recorded when they appear.

## How the frontier changes

Every time something new enters the brain, Claude says whether any of these hypotheses got stronger or weaker, and why. A new hypothesis comes in when a pattern points somewhere that is not here yet.
